    ABOUT THE PLATFORM

    Learning that adapts to you

    AI Personal Teacher is an adaptive AI-powered learning platform designed to make education more personalized, interactive, and easier to understand.

    Instead of giving every student the same lesson, the platform adapts explanations, questions, and learning activities based on the student's progress and responses.

    1.Personalized Learning

    Lessons are generated around the student's subject, topic, learning level, goals, and available study time. This helps create a learning experience that is relevant to each student's needs.

    2.Adaptive AI Teaching

    The AI teacher evaluates student responses and identifies areas where the student may be struggling. When an answer is incorrect, it can provide a different explanation, example, analogy, or follow-up question instead of simply showing the correct answer.

    3.Grounded Learning

    Students can upload study material such as PDFs. The platform uses the provided learning content to support lesson generation, helping students learn from their own academic material.

    4.Continuous Assessment

    Understanding is checked through questions and assessments. Student performance is used to track progress, identify strengths and weaknesses, and guide what should be learned next.

    05

    Interactive Learning Media

    Difficult concepts can be supported with AI-generated visuals, teacher voice, and learning videos. Multiple forms of content make lessons more engaging and can help students understand concepts more clearly.

    06

    Learning Progress

    The platform keeps track of completed lessons, assessment performance, and learning progress so students can understand where they are and continue learning from where they stopped.

    HOW IT WORKS

    From study material to personalized learning

    AI Personal Teacher combines student information, learning content, assessment results, and AI-generated teaching to create an adaptive learning cycle.

    1.Understand the Student

    The platform considers the student's learning preferences, subject, goals, and current progress.

    2. Create a Learning Path

    Topics are organized into a structured learning path so students can progress step by step.

    3.Teach and Interact

    The AI teacher explains concepts, asks questions, and responds to the student's understanding.

    4. Adapt and Improve

    Student responses are used to provide additional explanations, follow-up questions, and guidance when needed.

    TECHNOLOGY

    Built with modern AI technology

    The platform combines a FastAPI backend, Supabase for data and authentication, Gemini for AI-powered teaching and content generation, and retrieval-augmented learning for working with uploaded study material.
